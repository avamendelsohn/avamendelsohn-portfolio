About

I'm a Senior Experience Designer at Autodesk, with a background in human factors engineering, architecture and studio art.

I've been at Autodesk since July of 2024. My art background and training in human factors shape how I work: I start with how people actually use something, then design with aesthetic and craft at the forefront.

Outside of work I love to make things by hand: I build furniture, restore bikes, paint and print. I spend as much time outdoors as possible, biking, hiking, birdwatching or looking for the highest place to watch a sunset.

I'm always excited to talk about design, fabrication, the outdoors, or any sort of combination.
